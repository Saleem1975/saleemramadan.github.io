Industrial Digital Twin & Fault Analytics

A modular Python/Streamlit prototype for simulating two mixing tanks, monitored equipment, virtual sensors, fault scenarios, and live operational data.

Python Streamlit Plotly Simulation Fault Analytics CSV / JSON

Demo video of the working Streamlit prototype.

What I Built

  • Independent simulation of two mixing tanks with level and temperature dynamics.
  • Monitored pump, agitator motor, bearing, heater, and virtual-sensor behavior.
  • Configurable sudden, gradual, intermittent, random, and permanent fault profiles.
  • Live Plotly trends, equipment-status monitoring, synchronized logging, and CSV export.
  • Reusable JSON configurations and an interface architecture prepared for future industrial data adapters.
Download README

Analytics Value

The prototype creates synchronized normal and faulty operating data that can support condition monitoring, anomaly analysis, reliability studies, and future predictive-maintenance modeling.

Current Scope

This is an engineering prototype using simplified physics and equipment relationships. It is not presented as a calibrated or validated model of a specific industrial asset. Future work can connect real measurements and industrial communication protocols for operational calibration and deployment.
